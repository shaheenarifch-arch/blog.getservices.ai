1. AI Consulting: a clear roadmap before you spend

AI consulting at GetServices.ai is a short, fixed-scope engagement that answers one question: where will AI pay off first in your business, and what will it cost?

In week one we run an AI readiness review. We look at how work really flows through your business, which tools you already pay for, where data lives, and which tasks eat the most staff time. We also inventory any AI your team already uses on its own, because unapproved "shadow AI" is one of the biggest hidden risks in regulated industries. (We covered why in The Hidden Cost of AI Failures in Healthcare.)

By the end of week two you get a prioritized roadmap. Each opportunity is ranked by expected return, effort and risk, with a cost and timeline estimate, written without jargon. Many clients use the roadmap to build in-house; others hand it straight to our FDEs.

Good fit if: you have a budget for AI this year but no shortlist, or leadership keeps asking "what's our AI plan?"

2. AI Chatbots and Voice Agents: never miss a customer again

An AI chatbot or voice agent from GetServices.ai is an assistant trained on your own FAQs, documents, menus, price lists and policies. It answers customers around the clock, on your website, WhatsApp and SMS for chat, or on your phone line for voice.

The value is simple: the calls and messages you miss after hours, at lunch rush or on weekends are revenue walking to a competitor. A well-built agent:

  • answers routine questions instantly, in a consistent voice;
  • qualifies leads by asking the questions your best salesperson would ask;
  • books, moves and confirms appointments in your existing calendar;
  • hands off to a human, with the conversation summary attached, whenever it should.

That last point matters most. We design every agent with clear limits: what it may answer, what it must escalate, and what it must never say. In healthcare and legal settings, that means no clinical or legal advice, ever, and a clean hand-off to staff.

See examples on the AI Chatbots and AI Voice Agents pages.

3. AI Automation and Integrations: stop copying data between tools

AI automation removes the repetitive work that sits between your systems: re-typing form submissions into a CRM, building the same weekly report, chasing documents by email, or updating three tools when one thing changes.

Our FDEs build these workflows on proven automation platforms such as n8n, connected to the tools you already use. Common connections include Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Zapier, Jobber and Follow Up Boss, plus more than 100 others through standard APIs.

Typical automations we deliver:

  • Lead routing: new enquiries are enriched, scored and sent to the right person within seconds.
  • Document intake: invoices, forms and IDs are read, checked and filed without manual typing.
  • Reporting: weekly numbers are pulled from each system and written up in plain English.
  • Follow-ups: reminders, renewals and review requests go out on schedule, every time.

Every automation we hand over comes with a short runbook: what it does, who owns it, and what to check if something looks wrong. You should never depend on a workflow nobody understands.

Read more on the AI Automation and AI Integrations pages.

4. Custom AI Development: AI built on your own data

Off-the-shelf AI knows the internet. Custom AI knows your business. When a use case depends on your proprietary data, such as your contracts, product catalog, clinical protocols or historical tickets, we build a solution around it.

Depending on the problem, that can mean retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) so answers cite your own documents, fine-tuning for a specialist task, computer vision for images and scanned paperwork, or predictive models for demand and risk. Every custom build is tested against real scenarios before launch and monitored after it, because a model that works in a demo can still drift on live data.

Good fit if: you have a clear, valuable use case and data that a generic tool cannot see.

Details on the Custom AI Development page.

5. ATLAS AEO: get found and cited in AI answers

ATLAS AEO is GetServices.ai's answer engine optimization platform. It shows how visible your business is across search and AI assistants, then helps you fix what is holding you back.

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of making your website easy for AI assistants to read, trust and quote. Traditional SEO gets you a blue link on Google. AEO gets your business named in the answer itself when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude or Google's AI Mode for a recommendation. If you are new to the idea, start with our complete guide to AEO.

ATLAS works in three steps:

  1. Scan. ATLAS checks what search engines and AI assistants can actually read on your site. The free scan needs no account and no card.
  2. Analyze. You see your gaps, the competitors AI mentions instead of you, and your best opportunities.
  3. Improve. ATLAS agents help fix technical issues and publish answer-first content that is quality-checked before it goes live, then keep monitoring competitors and rankings.

Its main capabilities cover AI visibility analysis, technical SEO and AEO audits (broken links, meta tags, structured data, sitemaps and speed), content optimization, and continuous monitoring with alerts when rankings move.

6. AI Pods: role-based AI workers, coordinated by Atlas

An AI Pod is a pre-scoped AI worker built around one business role rather than one task. Instead of buying a chatbot here and an automation there, you hire a pod for a job, and it arrives with the workflows, integrations and guardrails that job needs.

Every pod is coordinated by Atlas, the orchestration layer. Atlas routes tasks between pods, sequences multi-step work, inserts human-in-the-loop checkpoints where a person must approve, and keeps an audit trail of every action. That orchestration is included with every pod.

8. BLACK Edition: executive AI that picks the right model for you

BLACK is the premium executive edition of GetServices.ai. It is built for founders, executives and operators who want strong answers without managing five different AI subscriptions.

Behind BLACK sits RAMBO, which stands for Request Analysis & Model Brokering Orchestrator. You write one brief. RAMBO decides which engine handles each part of it, GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or Perplexity, and assembles one polished output, with automatic fallback if a provider is down.

For decisions that matter, Executive Mode sends your brief to several models at once and shows you where they agree and where they disagree, instead of hiding the conflict. Every candidate answer and the routing trail stay available on demand.

Typical BLACK briefs include board reports, portfolio reviews, market scans and executive decision memos. Connectors for tools such as ServiceNow SPM, Jira, Salesforce, Google Drive, Excel and Power BI are rolling out. New accounts start with 50 free credits, then move to a paid plan.

9. itsmPRO: AI-native IT service management, run in plain words

itsmPRO is GetServices.ai's AI-native IT service management (ITSM) platform. It covers the core work of an IT service desk, incidents, user requests, problems, changes, a service catalog and a configuration management database (CMDB), and lets you operate it by typing or speaking in plain English.

It was designed by people who have spent years building enterprise ITSM, for the small and mid-sized teams those enterprise tools price out. Highlights include:

  • Floppy, the built-in AI assistant, on every screen. Ask "how many P1 incidents?" or "reassign this ticket to Deskside," and anything that changes data waits for your Approve.
  • Change-failure detection that links a new incident to a recently completed change on the same configuration item and scores the match from 0 to 100, so you find the cause faster.
  • Agile boards, a flow designer, integrations, a REST explorer, reports, dashboards and a self-service portal.
  • Builder Studio, where you describe what you want and the AI builds it, with approval before anything goes live.

Asset management and strategic portfolio management (SPM) are on the roadmap as later phases. If you are moving from another ITSM tool, our FDEs can plan the migration with you.

How a GetServices.ai engagement works

Whether you start with a chatbot, an automation or a pod, the delivery path is the same four steps. Most clients go live within about two weeks.

  1. Discovery and scoping (day 1). We map your current workflow, agree on success metrics and confirm what the AI may and may not do.
  2. Build and train (week 1). FDEs build the solution on your data and connect it to your existing tools.
  3. Test and refine (week 2). We test against real scenarios, including the awkward ones, until it meets the agreed metrics.
  4. Deploy and support (ongoing). We launch, monitor performance and keep improving it. Support is included in every engagement.
What is an FDE?

A forward-deployed engineer works directly with your team, inside your real tools and constraints, instead of handing you software and walking away. It is the difference between buying a gym membership and hiring a trainer.

Why regulated industries choose GetServices.ai

A wrong answer from a restaurant chatbot costs a booking. A wrong answer in a clinic, law office or lender can cost a license. That is why our delivery is built around the same controls regulated organizations already trust:

  • Human approval where it counts. Atlas, ROMEO, Floppy and BLACK all pause for a person before high-impact actions.
  • Audit trails. Orchestrated actions are logged, so you can show an auditor who did what, when and why.
  • Least-privilege access. Agents get only the permissions their role needs, managed like any other system account.
  • Change discipline. New automations and model updates go through testing before they reach live data, the same way an IT change would.
  • Plain-English documentation. Every build ships with a runbook your team can actually read.

Under the hood, an integration and orchestration platform (IOP) acts as a single front door for our agents, checking access, routing each call to the right system through MCP servers, and logging every call. If MCP is new to you, the Model Context Protocol introduction is a good primer.

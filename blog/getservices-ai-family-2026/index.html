What is GetServices.ai?

Short answer: GetServices.ai is an AI services company headquartered in Boston that helps small businesses and regulated organizations put AI to work — through consulting, done-for-you automation and three purpose-built platforms. More about us.

Most small businesses do not need another AI chatbot. They need answers to practical questions: Why are customers finding my competitor in ChatGPT and not me? How do I stop losing leads from missed calls? Which of these hundred AI tools is actually safe to use with patient or client data? GetServices.ai exists to answer those questions and then do the work.

ATLAS AEO: the AI platform that brings organic traffic

Short answer: ATLAS AEO is an AI answer engine optimization platform. You bring your website; ATLAS audits it, fixes what it can, writes optimized articles and tracks how often search engines and AI assistants surface you.

Search has changed. A growing share of buyers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Claude for a recommendation before they ever click a search result. If your site is not structured so that AI systems can understand and cite it, you are invisible in those conversations — even if you still rank on page one of Google. That is the problem answer engine optimization (AEO) solves, and it is why we built ATLAS as the first AI AEO platform focused squarely on bringing real organic traffic.

What ATLAS does for your website

  • Audits and scores every page for technical SEO health, structured data, content clarity and AI-readiness.
  • Applies fixes automatically where it can — titles, meta descriptions, schema, canonical tags, internal links — and queues the rest for approval or for an FDE.
  • Writes articles fast. ATLAS's Rapid mode produces SEO- and AEO-ready articles with an AI-generated hero image in a fraction of the time older workflows needed.
  • Plans social distribution across LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube, Instagram and Reddit, learning from what performs.
  • Speaks your customers' languages with a multi-language interface.

How ATLAS is tested

We do not ask customers to trust an untested engine. ATLAS runs every day across our own portfolio of more than twenty live websites — travel, retail, wellness, automotive, education and more — managed from its own ATLAS account. Every improvement to the platform is proven on real traffic before it reaches a customer.

ROMEO Studio: the GetServices AI operating environment

Short answer: ROMEO Studio is a conversational AI workspace. You tell ROMEO what you need — a website, an app, a blog post, a social campaign, a daily task — and it plans, executes, shows its work live and asks for approval before anything important happens.

If ATLAS is a specialist, Studio is the whole workshop. It is the operating environment where every GetServices.ai capability comes together, with ROMEO as the universal interaction layer. ATLAS lives inside Studio as one flagship product; App Builder sits beside it as another; and new systems plug in without a rewrite.

The Studio north star

Every request in Studio follows the same path: Ask → Form → Execute → Observe → Adapt → Verify → Matter. You ask in plain language; ROMEO forms a plan; specialist pods execute; you watch progress in the Live Activity panel; ROMEO adapts if something changes; results are verified; and anything useful becomes Outcome Matter — a saved, reusable result you can build on later.

What you can do in Studio

  • Build websites and web apps. Describe what you want — or choose a custom design and let ROMEO ask clarifying questions through clickable options. You see each page as soon as it is ready, and finished sites publish to their own repository.
  • Grow with ATLAS. Onboard a website and run SEO/AEO audits, blog writing and social scheduling without leaving the conversation.
  • Run any AI pod. Ask for ORION, NOVA, BUBLU or any other specialist and ROMEO brings it up.
  • Automate daily work. Email drafting and sending, job posts, prompt writing, and code formatting and validation for Python, JavaScript, JSON and XML.
  • Stay in control. Approvals, execution history, recovery and an instant stop control keep you in charge.

Studio is designed to be easy: a clean, minimal home screen with a greeting, a prompt box and compact tiles; clickable buttons for every option; and a signed-in dashboard that tracks your journey from onboarding onward. You pay only for the credits you use.

BLACK Edition and the RAMBO router

Short answer: BLACK Edition is the premium executive edition of GetServices.ai. Its RAMBO router sends each request to the AI engine best suited to it — Claude, GPT, Gemini, Perplexity, NotebookLM or Grok.

Executives do not have time to decide which AI model is best for a board summary versus a market scan versus a portfolio risk review. BLACK Edition makes that decision for them. Behind its dark-and-gold executive interface sits RAMBO — Request Analysis & Model Brokering Orchestrator. RAMBO reads each request, weighs what it needs (reasoning depth, live web research, document grounding, speed) and brokers it to the right engine.

The BLACK landing experience shows these model agents working live in tabbed executive scenarios: drafting board reports, running strategic portfolio management and pulling together connected portfolio tools. It is built to stay lightweight, so it feels just as fast on a phone in an airport lounge as on a desktop in the boardroom.

BLACK Edition is a paid unlock that shares your login and credits with ROMEO Studio, so moving between the everyday workspace and the executive suite is seamless.

How the GetServices.ai family works together

Short answer: You talk to ROMEO, ROMEO calls the right pods and platforms, ATLAS handles visibility, RAMBO picks the best model for executive work, and our FDEs step in wherever a human should.

Here is a typical journey for a small Boston medical clinic:

  1. Advisory. An FDE runs an AI readiness session and identifies two priorities: missed patient calls and weak local search visibility.
  2. Automation. We deploy an AI receptionist and lead-routing workflow so every call and form gets a fast, compliant response.
  3. Visibility. The clinic's website is onboarded to ATLAS AEO. MAYA applies technical fixes, ATLAS publishes question-led articles, and TITAN strengthens the trust signals AI assistants look for.
  4. Growth. ORION turns top articles into short videos, while NOVA reports weekly on traffic, calls and AI citations.
  5. Leadership. The practice director uses BLACK Edition to produce a monthly board-ready summary, with RAMBO choosing the best engine for each section.

Every step runs inside the same account, the same credits and the same approval rules. That is the advantage of a family rather than a pile of disconnected tools.

The same pattern works for a restaurant that wants fewer missed reservation calls, a law firm that wants to be the firm ChatGPT names for local estate planning, or a real estate team that wants listing content and social posts handled every week. The pods change; the system around them stays the same.

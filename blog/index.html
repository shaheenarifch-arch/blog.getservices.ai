Before you choose an AI tool

Three checks that save most small businesses time and money.

Confirm pricing on the vendor's sitePlans, limits and free tiers change often. Check the current price page before you sign up.
Check data and compliance termsIn healthcare, legal or finance, ask where your data is stored and whether the vendor will sign the agreements you need (for example a HIPAA BAA).
Start with a short trialTest the tool on one real task first, and read the cancellation terms before you commit.

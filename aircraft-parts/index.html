Questions

What paperwork comes with a part?

Each part ships with release documentation that matches its condition and history, typically an FAA Form 8130-3 or EASA Form 1 for new, overhauled or repaired parts, plus traceability back to the manufacturer or last operator and a non-incident statement where available. We send copies with the quote so you can check them before you buy.

Do you repair or certify parts?

No. We are a broker. Parts come from suppliers and FAA-certificated repair stations; the release certificate is issued by them. Your maintenance organization decides whether a part is eligible to install.

What condition codes do you quote?

NE (new), NS (new surplus), OH (overhauled), SV (serviceable), RP (repaired) and AR (as removed). Tell us which you accept.

Can you help with AOG?

Yes. Mark the request as AOG and include the part number, quantity and delivery airport, and call 313-729-1534 so we start sourcing straight away.

How do I sell surplus stock?

Send your part numbers, quantities, condition and the paperwork you hold. We match them to open requests from buyers and quote you before anything ships.

GetServices.ai is a parts broker, not a repair station or manufacturer, and does not issue airworthiness release certificates. Installation eligibility is decided by the installing maintenance organization. Help: FAA: suspected unapproved parts program.
